# Talley & Talley Law Firm

Build a complete, premium, multi-page website for Talley & Talley, attorney Thomas Talley, Hobart, Oklahoma. Phone: (580) 726-2251.



IMPORTANT: Build a complete website with separate working pages, NOT a one-page website.



Create these pages: Home, About the Firm, Practice Areas, Real Estate Law, Probate & Estate Administration, Family Law, Wills & Estate Planning, and Contact.



Design: High-end American law firm aesthetic inspired by established US legal practices. Deep navy, warm white, restrained gold accents, elegant typography, professional courthouse and legal imagery, generous whitespace, strong visual hierarchy, premium header and footer.



Homepage: Impressive hero with attorney-focused messaging, prominent Call Now button, practice-area cards, firm introduction, reasons to choose the firm, and a polished closing CTA.



Interior pages: Each page must have its own unique hero, relevant content, appropriate imagery, clear section structure, and contextual calls to action. Use reusable components but do not duplicate identical layouts and paragraphs across every page.



Animations: Slow, sophisticated scroll-triggered fade-in and upward text reveals, subtle staggered section entrances, smooth scrolling, refined hover effects, and elegant transitions. Avoid fast or distracting animations.



Functionality: Working multi-page navigation, responsive mobile menu, clickable phone links, mobile sticky Call Now button, SEO titles and descriptions, accessible contrast, and fast loading. Keep the contact page simple with phone and address; do not spend time building a complex contact form or backend.



Use only verified firm information. Do not invent attorney credentials, awards, client testimonials, or case results. Make the final result feel custom-designed, established, trustworthy, and impressive enough to present to a paying US client. Build the actual pages and complete their layouts, not just a homepage mockup.
